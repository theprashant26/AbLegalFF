Our Culture

A collaborative, rigorous and humane workplace — where every member of the team is trusted to contribute.

Our Ethos

Excellence is a habit, not an act.

At Ab Initio, the way we work with each other shapes the way we work for our clients. The same three principles that define our client service — Trust, Timely & Prompt Execution and Transparency — define our workplace. Partners lead from the front in court, doors stay open, and every associate and intern is encouraged to take ownership, ask questions and argue their point — on paper and before the bench.

What We Stand For

Integrity

Collaboration

Matters are staffed as teams; knowledge is shared freely across practices and levels.

Learning

Discussions on landmark judgments, drafting workshops and research-driven newsletters.

Respect

A professional, inclusive and respectful environment, compliant with POSH norms.

Learning & Growth

How We Grow Lawyers

A structured path from intern to independent counsel, anchored in hands-on experience.

  • Research & drafting — memos, pleadings and legal opinions, reviewed closely by seniors
  • Courtroom exposure — Supreme Court, Delhi High Court, NCLT, NCLAT and tribunals
  • Client interaction — conferences, strategy sessions and cross-examination preparation
  • Independent practice — arguing matters and leading files
  • Thought leadership — contributing to newsletters and publications
