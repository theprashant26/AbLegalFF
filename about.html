Who We Are

Ab Initio means “from the beginning” — and that is how we approach every matter.

Established in 2016, Ab Initio Legal is a full-service law firm based in New Delhi, with associate offices across the country. The firm carries diversified expertise in Dispute Resolution, Commercial & Corporate Litigation, International and Domestic Arbitration, Insolvency, Delhi High Court (Original & Appellate Side), Blue & White-Collar Crimes, Civil & Criminal Trials, Writs and Appeals, Consumer matters and SLPs before the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India. Our lawyers bring a cumulative experience of more than five decades, with seamless cross-practice expertise.

Our Mission

Our practice is driven by Partners with varied experience across judicial and quasi-judicial fora in different jurisdictions. We aim to provide our clients a cost-effective, result-oriented and coordinated approach to the unpredictability attached to litigation and dispute resolution.

Litigation can be a challenging and resource-intensive process; our team is dedicated to minimising risk and maximising outcome. By combining strategic foresight, meticulous preparation and practical advice, we ensure our clients are equipped to face the uncertainties of litigation.

We offer flexible billing arrangements — retainer, hourly or fixed fee — so that clients have clarity and predictability in their legal expenses. The Three T’s: Trust, Timely & Prompt Execution and Transparency are the founding principles of our practice.
