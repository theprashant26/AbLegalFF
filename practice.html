    Frequently Asked Questions

    Got a question about how we work? Here are answers to some common ones.

    How do I schedule a consultation?

    Call us on 011-40393888 / +91-8800808022, write to communications@abinitiolegal.in, or use the form below. We respond promptly.

    Which courts and tribunals do you appear before?

    The Supreme Court of India, the High Court of Delhi and other High Courts, District and Commercial Courts, NCLT/NCLAT, Consumer Commissions, RERA, DRT/DRAT, Labour Courts, CAT and arbitral tribunals including DIAC.

    How are your fees structured?

    We offer flexible billing arrangements — retainer, hourly or fixed fee — depending on the nature and complexity of the matter, so that clients have clarity and predictability in their legal expenses.

    Do you handle matters outside Delhi?

    Yes. The firm is based in New Delhi with associate offices across the country, and represents clients before forums in different jurisdictions.

    Is the information I share confidential?

    Information shared through this website is not treated as confidential until an advocate–client relationship is formally established. Please avoid sharing sensitive details in the initial message.

    What is Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR)?

    ADR includes arbitration, mediation and negotiation — methods of resolving disputes outside court. We recommend the best-suited mechanism after analysing each matter.
